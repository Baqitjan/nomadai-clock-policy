This app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC. The following ad formats are used:

AdMob may collect certain data from your device (such as advertising ID and device identifiers) to serve ads. This allows the app to remain completely free of charge.

Free Content Model

This app is completely free. No purchases or payments are required at any point. Additional clock styles can be unlocked by voluntarily watching short rewarded advertisements. All core features are available to every user at no cost.

Data Retention and Offline Work

This app works primarily offline and does not use any external cloud storage. No user data is retained by the developer. Your app settings (e.g., color theme, time format, selected clock style) are stored locally on your device only.
